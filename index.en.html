GOOD TO KNOW / 07

A few things to know
before you shoot.

Is XianNow a photo-editing app?

XianNow is an iOS camera and creative app. Frames combine a viewfinder, foreground graphics, text, and filters so you can preview a different look while you shoot.

Are my photos or characters uploaded to the cloud?

The current version has no XianNow account, cloud backup, or cross-device sync. Photos, personal frames, and characters are mainly managed on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details about weather and analytics data.

Are the example images real user submissions?

No. The photos and finished frame examples on this site are AI-generated visual examples, not real user submissions. Actual results vary with device, lighting, and settings.

How can I send feedback?

Open the “XianNow” page in the app and choose “Feedback & Suggestions.” Tell us which screen you were on, what you did, and what you expected. Development builds do not automatically upload photos, location, or diagnostic logs.
