## When is my free event used?

Editing a draft or previewing templates does not use the allowance. **Your free event is used when its first photo is successfully saved inside the app.** There is no photo-count limit within that event; you can leave and restart the app to continue it.

The allowance is one event in total, not one per month. After ending and archiving it, creating or duplicating another event requires Pro. Deleting an event does not return the allowance. Existing photos remain available to view and export.

## How do I adjust templates?

Choose among the same five scenes when creating an event or browsing templates: tea and coffee gatherings, creative workshops, lifestyle markets, sharing salons, and outdoor gatherings. A recommended color style is already set. You can change the text, logo and filter intensity, or restore the recommended settings.

Basic templates and these edits are available for your first event. Guidance can be skipped, and help is available inside the app. **The app interface is currently in Simplified Chinese.**

## A photo is missing from Apple Photos. What should I do?

Check the photo and its save status inside the app first, then check permission to add photos in iOS Settings. If saving to Apple Photos fails, the completed local photo stays inside the app so you can retry later.

If the app says the system save status is uncertain, check Apple Photos before retrying to avoid duplicates. Do not immediately uninstall the app or clear its data. Export important local photos first.

## How do I restore Pro?

Use the App Store account that made the purchase, then tap **恢复购买** (Restore Purchases) in the membership page or settings. Stay connected while Apple verifies the purchase. You do not need to register an app account.

Valid one-time, monthly and annual purchases can be restored, including existing purchases. If no valid purchase is found, check the account on your Apple receipt and any subscription expiry date.

## What is the difference between a one-time purchase and a subscription?

A valid subscription and a one-time purchase unlock the same Pro features, including starting more events and reusing your event style. A one-time purchase does not renew automatically. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew for the selected period until cancelled.

Available plans, price, currency and renewal terms appear in the app and on Apple's purchase confirmation. **Apple displays the actual local price.** Existing purchases retain their original purchase type. Future separate apps, cloud services or pay-per-use services are not included. Plans do not automatically credit or refund one another.

## How do I cancel or request a refund?

Use **管理订阅** (Manage Subscriptions) in the app or [Apple subscription management](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) to cancel renewal. Apple's page shows the remaining validity period. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Submit refund requests through [Apple's refund service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). Apple handles the decision.

## What happens after expiry or a refund?

You can still view and export existing photos and keep local templates. An event that already has its first photo saved can be finished without a paywall interrupting saving. Starting another Pro event requires a valid entitlement. Revoked and refunded purchases follow Apple's verified status.

## Will deletion or reinstalling reset my free event?

Deleting an event does not reset the allowance. Its record is separate from photos and stored in a local file and the device Keychain. Keychain records may remain after reinstalling, so **reinstallation does not guarantee another free event**.

The local allowance is not guaranteed to sync across devices. Apple purchase restoration is separate. The app does not provide cloud photo backup; reinstalling cannot guarantee recovery of unexported local photos.

## Can I shoot offline?

Built-in and cached templates and assets remain usable. New downloads, product prices, purchases and restoration need a working connection and the relevant services. Pro access follows the entitlement already verified on the device; permanent offline access in every circumstance is not promised.

## Are my photos uploaded? What does deletion remove?

Ordinary photos, Live Photo clips, sound, text, logos and face processing remain on your device and are not automatically uploaded to the developer. Sharing, iCloud Photos and authorized administrator publishing have separate processing paths described in the [Privacy Policy](/event-photo/en/privacy/).

When deleting a photo, choose whether to remove only the app copy or also its corresponding Apple Photos copy with the required permission. iCloud, Recently Deleted and copies shared to other services are managed separately. Uninstalling does not automatically delete those copies or cancel subscriptions.
